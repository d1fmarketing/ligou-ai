# Deferred voice benchmark backlog

This backlog preserves useful evaluation ideas without making them V0.1 launch
dependencies. Do not resume an item merely because its earlier plan exists.

## V0.2 — regression depth after pilot evidence

| Item | Resume when | Intended evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Expand from 12 to the 50-scenario corpus | Real pilot failures reveal the highest-value categories | Stable manifests covering the observed risk distribution. |
| Human calibration set | Consent, storage, and reviewer protocol are approved | Clearly labeled human recordings separate from synthetic results. |
| Targeted synthetic audio | A repeatable acoustic failure needs isolation | Small deterministic fixtures tied to that failure, not a general audio factory. |
| Regression history | At least two comparable release candidates exist | Versioned pass/fail, tool, latency, cost, and disqualification deltas. |

## V0.3 — model/provider research

| Item | Resume when | Intended evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Generalized provider adapters | A real fallback or cost alternative is deployable | Same input/evidence contract across actively viable candidates. |
| Multi-provider tournament | A product decision truly depends on provider choice | Authorized, budget-capped comparison using current official identities. |
| Human-scoring calibration | Deterministic business gates no longer explain quality differences | Inter-rater agreement and a documented adjudication protocol. |
| Sophisticated scorers and dashboards | Repeated benchmark runs create an operational review burden | Traceable reports that never override hard business failures. |

The dated provider research already in this branch is input to these later
decisions; it is not evidence that a provider passed a live Ligou call.
